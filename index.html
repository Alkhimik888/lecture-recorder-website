Why this one

No accounts. No keys. No tracking.

Four things this product happily refuses — so you can install, click, and forget it's there.

01

Zero setup

No sign-up, no email, no API keys, no settings screen to fight with. Install the extension, click the icon, hit Start recording.

02

Unlimited recording

Two-hour lecture, three-hour session — same file, same quality. AI Notes are free up to 300 min / month; the recording itself is always unlimited.

03

Audio gone in 60 seconds

For AI Notes your audio briefly visits our server, gets transcribed, then it's deleted within a minute. Recording without AI Notes never leaves your machine.

04

Fully anonymous

No name, no email, no profile — usage tracked only by an anonymous install ID on your device. Zero outbound requests during recording.

Privacy

The short version.

The media file is captured by Chrome's built-in tabCapture API and written straight to your Downloads — we never see it. AI Notes audio is sent over HTTPS, transcribed, and deleted within 60 seconds.
